Which beams

Connect and plan to see what the model holds.

Mesh

Sizes are in the model’s own length unit. Matching the beam stiffness adjusts plate thicknesses smoothly, by a few percent, so the mesh has the same area, centroid and second moments as the section.

Write

Deleting a beam also deletes its beam loads, so the loads are written onto the plates first. Anything a beam carries that a plate cannot — tendons, lanes, stage section changes — is listed under Left behind before you write.

Sections in the selection

Two checks, and they mean different things. Read compares the outline this plugin read against the properties CIVIL NX publishes — a miss means the dimensions were read wrongly. Plates compares the plate model against the section — a few percent is idealisation, not error. Correct any dimension and both rerun.
